Privacy policy

lumento LOAD

LOAD sends your photos and videos from your iPhone to your own NAS. Nothing goes to me or to anyone else. I have no server, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What LOAD uses, and where it goes

Your photo library

LOAD reads the photos and videos you choose to archive and uploads the original files to lumento STORE on your own NAS. Originals include whatever metadata the camera saved in them, such as date, time and, if location services were on, location. They go to your NAS and nowhere else.

Your local network

LOAD looks for lumento STORE on your network so it can pair with it. It does not scan for or talk to anything else.

Pairing details

When you pair, LOAD tells your STORE this iPhone's name, its iOS version and an identifier that iOS gives this developer's apps on this device. This lets your STORE recognise the phone. If you type a name under the pairing code, that goes to your STORE too, and you can change or clear it in Settings.

Notifications

LOAD's notifications are created on your iPhone. LOAD does not register for push notifications, and no notification server is involved.

On your iPhone

LOAD keeps its pairing credentials and your STORE's certificate fingerprint in the iOS Keychain, set never to sync to iCloud, and a record of upload progress in a local database. These stay on the phone. "Forget This Server" in Settings removes the pairing, and deleting the app removes the rest.

What I receive

Nothing. The connection runs directly between your iPhone and your NAS. It is encrypted, and LOAD checks it against the certificate your STORE presented when you paired. I have no copy of your photos, your pairing details or anything else, and no way to get one.

LOAD contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Its one outside library, GRDB, is a database that runs entirely on your iPhone.

Apple

Apple distributes LOAD through the App Store and processes data under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can turn this off under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Your data, your NAS

Everything LOAD uploads is stored on hardware you own and is under your control. Who can see it there depends on how you have set up your NAS and lumento STORE.

Children

LOAD collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If LOAD ever starts sending data anywhere other than your own NAS, this page will say so before that version is released.
