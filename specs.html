lumento ONE

What ONE needs and where it stops.

Your gallery on the NAS: what it runs on, which files it shows, and its limits.

WhatLimit
NAS neededAn Asustor NAS running ADM 5.1.0 or newer. Runs on x86-64 and 64-bit ARM models. More
Network portsONE uses ports 20904 (HTTP) and 20907 (HTTPS). Another app on the same ports will clash.
Library foldersUp to 100 folders. A curator adds them from a picker in Settings. More
Where a folder can beInside a share (for example /volume1/Photos/...). Not a whole volume, not ADM’s system folders, not another person’s home folder. More
Folder path lengthUp to 4096 bytes.
Folder display nameUp to 100 characters.
Photo typesJPEG, PNG, HEIC, HEIF, FlashPix. Camera RAW: ARW, NEF, CR2, DNG (shown from the preview inside the file). More
Canon CR3Listed, but shows a placeholder tile for now. More
Photo types not shownOther RAW brands (ORF, RW2, RAF and more), WebP, GIF, TIFF, AVIF, BMP.
Very large photosA photo over 100 megapixels, or a file over 256 MiB, shows a placeholder tile.
Video typesMP4, M4V, MOV, AVI, MTS, M2TS, MKV, WMV, MPG, MPEG, and DVD-Video folders.
How video playsPlays as-is when your screen can play the file; otherwise ONE changes the container or converts it when you press play. More
Converted videoConverted video is H.264 up to 1080p. Frame rates above 30 are halved. Originals that play as-is keep their size and frame rate. More
Conversions at onceOne at a time. A curator can raise it to 4. More
Viewing-copy cacheConverted videos and large-photo copies are kept up to 50 GB and for 30 days after last use. A curator can change both. Thumbnails aren’t counted. More
Free space keptONE keeps the larger of 10 GiB or 5% of the volume free, and stops converting below that. A curator can set it. More
Items per projectUp to 250 marked photos and videos together in one project. More
ProjectsUp to 500 projects. A name is 1 to 60 characters and can’t repeat. More
Project zipPhotos only (videos stay on the list). Capped at 250 MB by default; a curator can set 10 MB to 2 GB. Photos that don’t fit are named in list.txt. More
Who can sign inPeople with an account on the NAS. Two roles: Viewer (browse and watch) and Curator (also manage). Curators are the NAS administrators. More
Staying signed in30 days per device; up to 50 devices per account (the 51st signs out the oldest).
Curator changesCurators type their password again after 15 minutes idle.
TV remote linkEnds 10 minutes after the TV stops checking, or 4 hours after the last press.
lumento STORE

What STORE needs and where it stops.

The archive on your NAS: what it runs on, how it names files, and the limits you can hit.

WhatLimit
NAS neededAn Asustor NAS running ADM 5.1.0 or newer, installed from App Central. Runs on x86-64 and 64-bit ARM models. Other brands aren’t supported yet. More
Network portsPhones and the admin page use 20903 (HTTPS). More
Archive folderYou pick one existing folder inside a volume (for example /volume1/Photos), never a bare volume. STORE doesn’t create it. More
Where STORE writesOnly inside that folder: _lumento, the destination folders you add beside it, and one small hidden marker file. More
DestinationsUp to 500, nested up to 8 levels. A name is one folder name, up to 255 bytes, with none of / \ : * ? " < > |, and can’t be _lumento, default, #recycle or a bare 2026.08.
File namesCapture date and time to the millisecond, original extension kept, for example 2026.08.30 13.46.33.123.HEIC, in a month folder. A clash gets (2), (3). More
Estimated datesAn “e” after the time means the date couldn’t be read from the file. More
Existing filesSTORE never renames, moves or deletes a file it saved. Changing naming settings affects new files only. More
Pairing a phoneStart on the admin page. The code is 6 digits, works for 5 minutes and for one try. Pairing works only on your home network. More
Wrong pairing codes25 wrong tries from one address in 24 hours locks it out for 24 hours (7 days if it happens again). More
Admin passwordAt least 15 characters. Set the first one within 5 minutes of STORE starting, from your home network. More
Admin loginSigns out after 15 minutes idle and after 4 hours at most. 10 wrong passwords in 15 minutes lock that address out for 15 minutes. More
When the disk is nearly fullSTORE refuses new uploads when saving would leave less than 500 MiB free. Phones carry on by themselves when space returns. More
Largest file512 GiB per file.
Card importAn admin can turn it off. More
Admin pageOpens only from your home network.

lumento LOAD

What LOAD needs and where it stops.

Your iPhone, loaded to the NAS: what it needs and the limits you can hit.

WhatLimit
iPhone neededAn iPhone with iOS 18 or later. iPhone only (not iPad), portrait.
Photos accessNeeds full access to your photos (“All Photos”). With selected photos only, the overnight backup won’t run. More
What gets loadedPhotos, videos and Live Photos. Audio and other items are skipped. Hidden photos are never loaded. Photos from shared albums and photos synced from a computer aren’t loaded. More
BurstsEvery frame of a burst is loaded. More
EditsAn edited photo or video arrives beside the original. Apple’s edit data isn’t copied. More
Files per photoUp to 4 files for one photo (a Live Photo with an edit is 4). Anything beyond that is left out, and the photo is reported as only partly reached.
Slo-mo, Cinematic, Portrait, ProRAWLoad like any other photo or video; no special handling. More
Photos only in iCloudLOAD downloads them first and tells you the count before the Load. More
Free space on the phoneA Load pauses when the phone has under 500 MB free, and carries on by itself when there’s room. More
NetworkPhone and NAS must be on the same network. LOAD asks iOS for Local Network permission to find the NAS. No away-from-home access. More
Pairing code6 digits, typed as 123 456. Made on the NAS, works 5 minutes, one try. More
NAS per phoneA phone is paired with one NAS at a time. Pairing again replaces it, and the next Load only sends what’s missing. More
Overnight backupOff until you turn it on. Runs when the switch is on, the phone is charging, LOAD is in the background, you’ve allowed full photo access, and you’ve done one Load. iOS chooses when; there’s no fixed time. More
A Load that stallsA Load with no file landing for 48 hours stops by itself. More
Freeing up spaceLOAD never deletes from your phone unless you start “Delete Uploaded Photos”, and then only photos it has just checked are safely on the NAS. They go to Recently Deleted (30 days, Apple’s rule). STORE never deletes archived files. More
AccountNone. No sign-in. More

lumento KEEP

What KEEP needs and where it stops.

Your NAS, watched for failed backups and silent damage: what KEEP needs and the limits you can hit.

WhatLimit
NAS neededAn Asustor NAS running ADM 5.1 or newer. Runs on x86-64 and 64-bit ARM models.
What it watchesThe Photos share (/volume1/Photos) for now. Watching every share is next.
When it readsOnly inside a nightly window you pick, in the NAS’s local time, and nothing until you pick one. A window can cross midnight; the same start and end means all day.
Nightly read limitOptional, in GiB. Blank means no limit.
Staying out of the wayIt pauses while a RAID sync, a scrub or a backup runs.
Your filesIt only reads. It never repairs, renames, copies over or deletes a file, and the kernel blocks it from writing. It writes only its own index, pages and log lines.
File systemsIt warns about an ext4 volume, and an exFAT, FAT or NTFS backup disk, because they can’t catch damage by themselves. ext4 has no scrub.
NoticesIn ADM’s System log, once per change. KEEP checks that ADM will email you, but doesn’t send email itself.
When it warnsA backup with no success in 7 days, or in its schedule plus a day if that’s longer. A snapshot older than 2 days. A scrub or RAID check older than 35 days.
Locked sharesA locked encrypted share isn’t checked until you unlock it.
Admin pageHTTPS on port 20816, on your home network. Don’t forward it to the internet.
Admin passwordAt least 12 characters. A sign-in lasts 12 hours.
