The protocol

loadMENTO

The protocol LOAD and ONE use to talk to STORE. It checks what the NAS already has before sending anything, sends large files in 8 MB chunks, hashes every file at both ends, and never stores a file whose hash doesn't match.

The idea

Keep the storage.
Lose the friction.

NAS software can be powerful without feeling like infrastructure. lumento is intended to make the ordinary things—getting media onto your NAS, finding it again, and viewing it—feel light and uncomplicated. On your terms.

The first release is focused on Asustor. The family is designed so support can grow later without turning one app into everything.

This is not a replacement for your favorite media server. It's for those who want simplicity and reliability. Without docker, without postgres, without overhead.
