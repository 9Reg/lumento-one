The protocol

loadMENTO

The protocol LOAD and ONE use to talk to STORE. It checks what the NAS already has before sending anything, sends large files in 8 MB chunks, hashes every file at both ends, and never stores a file whose hash doesn't match.

lumentoFAMILY

Quiet infrastructure
for your memories.

Four apps, one job each.

LOAD brings your photos and videos in.
STORE secures them.
ONE shows them.
KEEP watches for damage.

Each one works on its own, and together they cover the whole journey from your phone to the screen in your living room.

Your NAS, your folders, organized as you wish. Your memories as you remember them, as you recorded them. Old and new, from FlashPix to old camcorders, from HEIC to 4K. Ready to view however you like, archived with confidence.

Think of it as infrastructure. It's yours and yours alone, or shared with your family. Use LOAD to get your photos to your NAS all at once, or overnight on a schedule. Never lose a photo when your phone dies. STORE tells LOAD when a photo or video is durable and you can safely delete it. KEEP helps prevent bit rot. ONE ties it all together in a media viewer. None of them need ONE, but together they're ONE lumento FAMILY.

Keep your media server. LOAD your memories. KEEP them safe. STORE them forever.
